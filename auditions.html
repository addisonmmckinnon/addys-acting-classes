Auditions 🎬

Don't worry — everyone who auditions gets a part! Auditioning isn't about whether you're "good enough," it's just how I decide:

  • Which part is the best fit for you in a musical or performance
  • Where to start you off if you sign up for private lessons

So come as you are and just have fun with it — there's no way to "fail" an audition here!
